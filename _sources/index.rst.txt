filamentum
==========

filamentum traces curved and crossing filaments in cryo-EM micrographs and writes RELION coordinates.

A small U-Net (0.24 M parameters) predicts two things for every pixel of a micrograph Fourier-cropped to 5.2 Å/px: the probability
that the pixel lies on a filament, and the direction of the filament axis. A tracer turns these maps into smooth curves that continue
through crossings. It exports them as straight start-end pieces, which any RELION version reads, and as polylines for RELION 5.1's
curved extraction.

The network is meant to be fine-tuned for each specimen on a few manually picked micrographs. The shipped starting network, the *pan
network*, was trained on Aβ42 and ten other filament data sets, and fine-tuning it takes about a minute on a GPU. Training,
tracing and micrograph selection all run as RELION ``External`` jobs, so picks, networks and coordinates stay in the RELION pipeline.
