Troubleshooting and questions
=============================

The progress line in ``run.out`` stays at 0 traced for a while
   Tracing finishes in waves: all tracing processes start a micrograph at the same time, and each takes from seconds to minutes,
   longer the more crowded the micrograph. Nothing finishes for a while, then a batch at once. The progress line is printed at
   least every minute; its rate is measured over the last 5 minutes, from the first finished micrograph on.

Tracing is slow
   Give the job more CPU cores and set *Number of threads* (``--j``) to the cores minus 4 for the reading processes; the job warns
   when it has fewer CPUs than processes. The GPU is rarely the limit. ``filamentum check-env`` times the steps on the machine.

The GUI number in the manual picker is not the trace length
   For a lines list, RELION's manual picker shows the number of coordinate rows, i.e. control points along the traces, not their
   length or the number of traces. ``n_traces`` and ``length_um`` are in ``progress.tsv`` and in the QC titles.

Micrographs have no traces although filaments are visible
   Check the QC overlay. If short traces were dropped, lower ``--min_trace_nm``; if the network misses the filaments, pick a few of
   those micrographs and fine-tune again. Micrographs with every trace shorter than ``--min_trace_nm`` look empty in the statistics.

Traces stop short of the micrograph edge
   By design: ``--border_nm`` (10 nm) keeps traces off the edge, where extraction boxes would be cut off. ``--border_nm 2`` traces
   almost to the edge.

Segments sit on crossings
   ``--avoid_crossings_nm R`` (e.g. 10) cuts traces at crossings and keeps every end at least R nm from other traces (at most 2R
   is removed from an end); compare the before/after close-ups in ``qc/avoid/`` of a QC run. It costs traced length on crowded
   micrographs.

The network was trained on data from another processing package
   A network records its preprocessing (target pixel size, ``--background_nm``) in its metadata, and tracing uses it. For
   micrographs from RELION's motion correction traced with a network trained on cryoSPARC micrographs, give ``--background_nm 10``.

``Permission denied`` when RELION starts the job
   The executable is not executable or not found. Use the installed ``filamentum-*-relion`` executables (on ``PATH`` or with their
   full path); in a git checkout the ``scripts/relion_external*.sh`` wrappers must have their executable bit set.

A run was interrupted
   Run the tracing job again in the same job folder with ``--resume`` added to its options; micrographs already in
   ``progress.tsv`` are skipped.

Coordinates for RELION 4.0
   ``--relion4`` writes the straight export with RELION 4's node type; extract with *Coordinates are start-end only?* = Yes.
