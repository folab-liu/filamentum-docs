Micrograph statistics

Every tracing job records one row per micrograph in progress.tsv. filamentum select filters on these columns, and both jobs summarise them in logfile.pdf and micrograph_stats.txt. Lengths are arc lengths along the traces.

n_traces

traces on the micrograph, i.e. paths in the lines export

length_um

their total length (µm)

median_trace_nm

median trace length (nm); 0 if there are no traces

max_trace_nm

longest trace (nm)

n_crossings

places where two different traces cross

crossings_per_um

n_crossings per µm of traced length

length_per_um2

traced length per µm² of micrograph: how crowded the micrograph is

straight_pieces

straight export: start-end pieces kept

straight_kept_um

straight export: length of the kept pieces (µm)

straight_dropped_um

straight export: length in pieces shorter than --min_piece_nm (µm)

What the statistics do and do not see

The statistics describe the exported traces only, after every tracing option has acted. Traces removed by --min_trace_nm (25 nm by default), parts within --border_nm of the edge, and parts cut by --avoid_crossings_nm are not counted. A micrograph on which every trace was shorter than --min_trace_nm therefore has n_traces = 0, like an empty one. To tell the two apart, trace again with a lower --min_trace_nm.

--min_trace_nm is not only a final filter in the default span tracer: it also decides which fitted stretches are kept and explained away while tracing, so a run with a different value can differ by more than the short traces.

Choosing limits

  • Look at the tracing job’s logfile.pdf first. The table gives percentiles, and the histograms show whether there is a separate group (e.g. a peak at zero for empty micrographs) or a long tail.

  • Statistics that move together carry the same information. On the example data n_traces and length_um have a Spearman correlation of 1.00, so a limit on either is enough. Crowding (length_per_um2) and crossings_per_um rise together, so a lower limit on length favours crowded micrographs unless it comes with an upper limit on crossings_per_um.

  • Check the result: the selection job’s log file shows the kept micrographs in colour, and its coordinate lists open the kept micrographs in the manual picker.

Typical selections:

drop micrographs with nothing traced

min = n_traces=1

enough filament to be worth extracting

min = length_um=1

avoid crowded micrographs

max = crossings_per_um=2 or length_per_um2=10

long filaments (e.g. for long-range work)

min = max_trace_nm=300

Tracing jobs made before 23 November 2026 recorded only n_traces, length_um and the straight export.
